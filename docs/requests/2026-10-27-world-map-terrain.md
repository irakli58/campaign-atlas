# The user's request of 27 Oct 2026: a massive expansion of the 2D world map's terrain

(Kept here as the user gave it, so that any session can work from it. Also asked the same day: every river rises in
the mountains, as in the real world, unless the game master draws it otherwise; and every map stays editable.)

I want you to significantly expand Atlas's 2D world map asset library, especially natural terrain and environmental features. Right now, I want much more variety in trees, forests, mountains, rivers, landscapes, and large geographical features, not just buildings and settlements. These assets should work across Fantasy, Modern, and Sci-Fi maps, with different visual styles appropriate to each setting. Before creating anything, inspect the existing asset library and avoid duplicating models that already exist.

## 1. Trees and Forests

Create a huge variety of trees and vegetation:

- Oak, pine, spruce, fir, birch, willow, maple, cedar, cypress, redwood, palm, acacia, baobab, cherry blossom, mangrove.
- Dead trees, burned trees, fallen trees, twisted trees, ancient giant trees.
- Individual trees, small clusters, groves, dense forests, sparse woodland.
- Temperate forests, boreal forests, tropical rainforests, jungles, bamboo forests, cloud forests.
- Swamp forests, mangrove forests, flooded forests.
- Autumn forests, snowy forests, dead forests, burned forests.
- Fantasy enchanted forests, glowing trees, corrupted forests, giant mushroom forests.
- Sci-Fi alien trees, bioluminescent forests, crystalline vegetation, fungal jungles, silicon-based plant formations.

Include large forest patches and modular forest edges, not just individual trees.

## 2. Mountains and Cliffs

Create many mountain types and formations:

- Individual peaks, twin peaks, mountain clusters, entire mountain ranges.
- Snow-capped mountains, rocky mountains, grassy mountains, desert mountains.
- Jagged alpine peaks, rounded ancient mountains, volcanic mountains.
- Active volcanoes, dormant volcanoes, volcanic craters.
- Canyons, ravines, gorges, mesas, plateaus, buttes.
- Cliffs, escarpments, ridges, rock faces, mountain passes.
- Glaciers, ice cliffs, frozen peaks.
- Fantasy floating mountains, cursed mountains, enormous stone formations.
- Sci-Fi alien mountain formations, crystal mountains, metallic cliffs, unusual planetary geology.

Include large connected mountain ranges that can stretch naturally across world maps, rather than repetitive individual mountain icons.

## 3. Rivers, Lakes, and Water

Create detailed water features:

- Small streams, creeks, brooks, rivers, wide rivers, enormous rivers.
- Straight, winding, branching, braided, and meandering rivers.
- River sources, tributaries, confluences, forks, deltas, estuaries.
- Waterfalls, rapids, cascades, river gorges.
- Ponds, lakes, great lakes, alpine lakes, crater lakes.
- Marshes, wetlands, swamps, bogs, floodplains.
- Fjords, bays, gulfs, lagoons, inlets, channels, straits.
- Islands, archipelagos, reefs, sandbars.
- Frozen rivers, frozen lakes, glaciers.
- Fantasy magical rivers, glowing lakes, cursed swamps.
- Sci-Fi acidic lakes, methane seas, lava rivers, alien liquid formations.

Rivers should be able to connect seamlessly, branch naturally, and follow the geography instead of looking like disconnected decorative lines.

## 4. Terrain and Landscapes

Add large-scale terrain assets for:

- Grasslands, plains, meadows, steppes, savannas.
- Rolling hills, highlands, valleys, basins.
- Sand deserts, rocky deserts, dunes, badlands.
- Tundra, permafrost, frozen wastelands.
- Beaches, coastlines, coastal cliffs.
- Farmland, orchards, vineyards, terraced fields.
- Volcanic fields, lava flows, ash-covered terrain.
- Salt flats, dry lakebeds, sinkholes.
- Caves, cavern entrances, massive craters.
- Fantasy magical wastelands, corrupted terrain, enchanted valleys.
- Sci-Fi alien deserts, toxic landscapes, crystalline plains, irradiated wastelands, extraterrestrial biomes.

## 5. Large Environmental Assets

I especially want massive assets that cover entire regions, not just small individual objects. Examples: entire mountain chains; huge forest regions; large swamp systems; river networks with tributaries; large deserts with multiple dune formations; giant volcanic regions; extensive glaciers and ice fields; massive canyons; entire island chains; large alien ecosystems; huge ruined or corrupted landscapes. Make these modular and editable so the DM can extend, reshape, combine, or partially erase them.

## 6. Different Styles for Fantasy, Modern, and Sci-Fi

- Fantasy: hand-drawn medieval cartography, parchment-style forests, illustrated mountains, beautiful rivers, and detailed fantasy landscapes.
- Modern: more geographically realistic terrain, natural vegetation patterns, modern atlas-style mountains and water systems.
- Sci-Fi: planet-specific terrain, unusual geology, alien vegetation, exotic environments, and futuristic cartographic presentation.

Don't simply recolor the same assets for every setting. Make them visually appropriate.

## 7. Procedural Generation and Natural Placement

I don't want Atlas to randomly scatter terrain icons everywhere. Improve generation so that:

- Mountains form believable ranges.
- Rivers originate from suitable terrain and flow toward lakes or seas.
- Forests form natural regions rather than random circles.
- Vegetation matches climate and geography.
- Deserts, mountains, rivers, and forests transition naturally.
- Terrain assets blend together without obvious seams.
- Large regions contain smaller details when zoomed in.

Allow the DM to manually place terrain, paint entire regions, and adjust generated landscapes.

## 8. Preserve Existing Features

Inspect Atlas's existing 2D asset library and map generator before implementing anything. Do not recreate existing assets unnecessarily. Do not replace functional maps with static images. Preserve existing map editing, zooming, navigation, and connections to 3D cities. You have creative freedom to choose the best approach, including modular assets, procedural terrain, brushes, reusable variations, and scalable illustrations.

The goal is to make Atlas capable of generating enormous, beautiful, richly detailed 2D world maps with realistic and varied terrain, rather than maps made from a handful of repeated icons.

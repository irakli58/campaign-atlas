One additional suggestion for Atlas's local AI narrator:
I want you to specifically investigate Kokoro TTS as the first option for Atlas's narration system, with Piper TTS as a lightweight alternative for users with less powerful computers.
Please research both and compare them with any newer or better local TTS solutions you discover.
My priorities are:

1. Natural voice quality: The narrator should sound like a real storyteller, with convincing pacing and intonation rather than robotic speech.
2. Fully offline operation: After downloading the model, narration should work without internet access or paid APIs.
3. Low hardware requirements: Ideally, narration should run reasonably well on ordinary computers, including systems without dedicated GPUs.
4. Multiple narrator voices: I want different voices for fantasy, horror, Sci-Fi, and general storytelling.
5. Easy installation: Users should be able to download and select narrator voices directly through Atlas rather than manually configuring Python environments.
6. Integration with the existing soundboard: Background music and ambience should automatically lower in volume while the narrator speaks.
7. Long-form narration: The narrator should handle long scene descriptions and campaign text with appropriate sentence breaks, pauses, and consistent pronunciation.
8. Licensing: Verify that the models and individual voice files can legally be distributed with Atlas, including for commercial use.

I also want you to investigate whether we could offer two narration modes:

* Performance Mode: A lightweight model that works quickly on almost any computer.
* Quality Mode: A more natural, expressive model for users with stronger hardware.

If Kokoro or Piper isn't the best solution, recommend something better and explain why.
Ideally, let me listen to several voice samples before selecting a narrator.
Please also investigate whether emotional narration is possible locally. For example, I want a horror narrator to sound tense or mysterious, while a fantasy narrator might sound dramatic or adventurous. Determine whether the model genuinely supports expressive delivery or whether a different model is necessary.
Finally, tell me whether we can integrate the selected TTS model directly into Atlas's current architecture without requiring a separate application.
Inspect the existing narrator implementation first, reuse whatever already works, and present your recommendations before implementing anything.
My ultimate goal is a free, high-quality, completely offline narrator that sounds as close to professional audiobook narration as reasonably possible.

# Request, 18 Nov 2026: consistency and ease of use

The user asked: "does everything look professional? and ready for million of people to use easily?". The answer was
not yet:
- many button styles side by side, and the pages dense;
- a new user faces a very large surface;
- no testing with real users;
- a 6 MB page;
- accessibility not audited.

The user, verbatim:

> i want consistency and also ease of use

The work this calls for:
1. **Consistency:** one design system. One kind of main button and one kind for everything else, a type scale, steady
   spacing, lighter borders and enough contrast, used the same way in every view.
2. **Ease of use:** a simpler first experience, with fewer things shown until they are needed, plain words, and clear
   main actions.

# Request, 18 Nov 2026: campaign-specific items

The user, verbatim:

> can you generate campaing specific items too?

What there was before: loot by rarity, quality and condition (engine/atlas-economy.js rollLoot), drawn from the age's
goods and the campaign's own `treasure` list. Nothing made a thing out of the campaign itself.

What is built: things made from the campaign's own material, each one:
- with a name and what it is;
- what it is made of;
- how it came to be, tied to the campaign's people, places, factions and history;
- what it does (magic only where the genre has it);
- who wants it, and where it can be found;
- its worth.

Each can be kept, given to the party, put at a place, and shown to the players.

# Canon sheet

The facts everything else must agree with. The Core comes from the user's story; the rest is brought up to date from the council's bible (`story/bible/`) before mapping anything, and kept open while writing.

## The Core (may not change)

What the user's story *is*, in five to ten plain sentences. The council may change anything but this.

1. 

## The story in five lines

1. What happened (the truth the players are trying to find).
2. Who did it and why.
3. What the players are asked to do at the start.
4. How it can end.
5. The tone.

## Where and when

The setting, the year, and (for the city map) the real city or the kind of invented one, with the quarter the story happens in.

## Invented while mapping (for the user to approve)

Everything added that touches the plot goes here, with the place it was added in. Small colour (a cat, a smell) does not need listing; a new clue, a new motive, a new relationship or a new date does.

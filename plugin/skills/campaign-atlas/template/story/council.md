# The council's sitting

How to hold it: `references/council.md` in the campaign-atlas skill. The user's story, exactly as given, is `source.md` beside this file. Four writers (Conan Doyle, Christie, Poe, Lovecraft) build the story; Tolkien judges it (`judgement.md`); it is finished only when all five agree.

## The Core

What may not change. Five to ten plain sentences.

## Round 2. Proposals

Numbered, concrete: named people, specific clues and where they lie, scenes, reversals, places.

## Round 3. The debate

Point by point: each position, the rejoinder, the clerk's ruling and its reason, any dissent. Every hole from Round 0 closed.

## Round 4. The build

Written out in `bible/`: the truth and its timeline; the antagonists' plan and clock; the cast; the clue web; red herrings; the places with their tiers; the acts; the endings; hooks and handouts.

## Round 5. The audit

Three clues to every conclusion; no bottleneck; choice at every stage; the idle table; a fair solution; something for each kind of player; the game's own rules; care.

## Round 6. The judgement

Tolkien's judgement is in `judgement.md`: his verdict on the whole, every chapter graded (Excellent, Good, Wanting, Poor) with what is good and why and what is not and why. The bar is Good.

## Round 7. The mending

Every point Tolkien graded Wanting or Poor: what the four changed, or why it stands (only the Core or a rule of play excuses it); then his judgement of the mended part. Repeat until nothing is below the bar.

## Round 8. The agreement

All five must agree on the finished story. Each says, in voice, whether they put their name to it, and if not, what would change their mind; an objection goes back to the mending until all five accept it.

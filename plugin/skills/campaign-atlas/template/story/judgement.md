# Tolkien's judgement

How to write it: `references/council.md`, section 8, in the campaign-atlas skill. He judges; he writes no plot. Every judgement has a grade (Excellent, Good, Wanting, Poor) and a reason. The bar is Good: anything below it is mended and judged again. He is not easily pleased.

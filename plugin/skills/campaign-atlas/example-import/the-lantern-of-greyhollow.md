# The Lantern of Greyhollow

An original adventure written for Campaign Atlas's importer tests, for four characters of 3rd level, for Dungeons & Dragons 5e. Free to use.

## Introduction

Greyhollow is a village in a valley where the fog never lifts. Thirty years ago the Abbess Veyra carried the Lantern of Saint Orsa into the abbey above the village and was never seen again. Since that night the dead have walked the valley road on moonless nights, and no one leaves. The Grey Brothers, who keep the abbey ruins, say the fog is a mercy. They are lying: their prior, Hollis, keeps the fog alive with the Lantern's stolen flame.

The adventure ends when the Lantern is relit at the abbey altar (the fog lifts), when Prior Hollis is destroyed or redeemed, or when the characters leave the valley with the Lantern (the fog follows them).

## Starting the adventure

The characters arrive at the Hanged Goose inn in Greyhollow at dusk, called by a letter from Mara Tell, the innkeeper, who begs for help: her brother Ansel went up to the abbey four days ago and has not come back.

## Chapter 1: The Lantern Reading

Before play, the DM draws three cards from an ordinary deck of six named cards: the Seer, the Hangman, the Mother, the Wolf, the Bell and the Key. The first card decides where the Lantern lies, the second where the Book of Saint Orsa lies, the third who will help the characters. Draw without putting cards back.

Where the Lantern lies (first card):
- The Seer: in the abbey crypt, area A4, in the Abbess's tomb.
- The Hangman: at the mill, area M2, under the millstone.
- The Mother: in the inn's cellar, area G2, bricked into the wall.
- The Wolf: in the abbey bell tower, area A3.
- The Bell: in the chapel of the abbey, area A2, on the altar under a shroud.
- The Key: carried by Prior Hollis himself.

Where the Book of Saint Orsa lies (second card):
- The Seer: with Old Wen, the hermit, in her hut.
- The Hangman: at the mill, area M1.
- The Mother: in Mara Tell's room at the inn, area G3.
- The Wolf: in the abbey crypt, area A4.
- The Bell: in the bell tower, area A3.
- The Key: in the abbey chapel, area A2.

Who will help (third card):
- The Seer: Old Wen, the hermit.
- The Hangman: Ansel Tell, if he is rescued.
- The Mother: Mara Tell.
- The Wolf: Brother Cole, a Grey Brother who doubts.
- The Bell: the ghost of the Abbess Veyra.
- The Key: no one; the characters stand alone.

## Chapter 2: Greyhollow

### The Hanged Goose (G)

G1. Common Room. A low room of smoke-black beams. Mara Tell keeps the bar. Villagers will speak of the walking dead, but never of the abbey.
G2. Cellar. Barrels and a wall of newer brick (see the Lantern Reading).
G3. Mara's Room. A narrow room above the kitchen. Mara keeps Ansel's last letter here: "The Brothers are not praying. They are feeding something."

Mara Tell, the innkeeper, is a stout woman of forty with flour on her sleeves; quick to laugh, quicker to worry. She wants her brother back. She knows the Grey Brothers buy far more lamp oil than any abbey needs. Secret: she once took coin from Prior Hollis to send travellers up the hill, and she is ashamed.

### The Mill (M)

M1. Grinding Floor. The mill has been silent for a year. Rats. A ledger shows the Brothers paid for flour in old abbey silver.
M2. The Wheel Pit. Cold water and a millstone that has not turned in a year.

Encounter at the mill: four zombies (stat block: Monster Manual) rise from the wheel pit if anyone goes down into M2 at night.

### Old Wen's Hut

A hut of turf and bone at the valley's edge. Old Wen, a blind hermit, was a novice at the abbey thirty years ago. She knows the Abbess went into the crypt with the Lantern and that Hollis followed her. She will trade what she knows for a song or a true secret.

### Random encounters on the valley road (d6, at night)

1-2: The walking dead: 1d4 zombies. 3: A Grey Brother carrying oil, who flees. 4: The fog thickens; travel takes twice as long. 5: A wolf that watches and does not attack. 6: The Abbess's ghost on the road, who points to the abbey and fades.

## Chapter 3: The Abbey of Saint Orsa (A)

A1. Gatehouse. A room 20 feet by 15 feet. A door in the north wall leads to the chapel (A2). Two Grey Brothers (cult fanatic stat blocks are too strong: use acolyte) bar the way unless the characters claim to be pilgrims.
A2. Chapel. 40 feet by 30 feet, with the altar against the north wall, where the Lantern must be relit. A shroud. An open archway in the east wall leads to the bell tower (A3); stairs in the southwest corner go down to the crypt (A4); a secret door in the west wall opens into the Prior's cell (A5). Prior Hollis preaches here at midnight.
A3. Bell Tower. A tower 15 feet square. The bell is cracked. From here the whole valley can be seen above the fog.
A4. Crypt. Below the chapel, 30 feet by 20 feet. The tomb of the Abbess Veyra. Her ghost rises if the tomb is opened, and attacks unless someone speaks her name.
A5. The Prior's Cell. A cell 10 feet square, a cot and a chest. The chest holds the Prior's silver and a potion of healing.

Prior Hollis, the leader of the Grey Brothers, is a gaunt man with a burned right hand, soft-spoken and patient. Use the priest stat block. He wants the fog to stay: in the fog, no one dies of age, and he is very old. He knows where the Lantern is only if the Key was drawn for it; otherwise he believes it lost. If attacked, he calls the dead from the crypt.

Brother Cole is a young Grey Brother with a stammer. He doubts the Prior and will open the gate at night if he is promised the fog will lift.

Ansel Tell is held in the crypt, starved but alive. He saw the Prior feed the stolen flame to the fog.

The Grey Brothers: the cult of the abbey, led by Prior Hollis, about a dozen strong; they hold the abbey and buy the village's oil.

## Treasure

The Lantern of Saint Orsa: a brass lantern with a cracked pane, unlit. A unique, rare wondrous item (homebrew): relit at the altar, it lifts the fog; carried, it keeps the walking dead at ten feet. The Book of Saint Orsa: the rite for relighting the Lantern; without it the rite takes a full night and a DC 15 Religion check. In the Prior's cell (A5): 120 gp in old abbey silver and a potion of healing.

## Endings

If the Lantern is relit, the fog lifts at dawn and the dead lie down. If the characters leave with the Lantern unlit, the fog follows them. If the Prior is redeemed (by Brother Cole's plea and the Book), he relights the Lantern himself and dies at dawn, at peace.
